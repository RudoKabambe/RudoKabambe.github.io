After Cyclone FreddyCould displaced families reach health care, and where could drones close the gap?

Cyclone Freddy displaced 19,426 people into 97 sites across Blantyre in March 2023. I combined Ministry of Health, IOM, population and travel-time data to find the sites that roads and clinics were failing, then designed a medical drone network to reach them.

The problem

After the floods and landslides, families sheltered in schools, churches and camps. IOM's site survey recorded who was there, but its "distance to nearest health centre" column was mostly empty. Responders needed to know which sites were cut off from care, and whether medical drones could reach them.

Data
  • Ministry of Health Health Facility Registry, February 2023: 146 mapped facilities in Blantyre.
  • IOM Displacement Tracking Matrix, Cyclone Freddy Round 1: 97 sites with GPS, population and health indicators.
  • Kontur 400 m population hexagons, the CPAS 100 m motorised travel-time raster, and OCHA boundaries down to ward and TA level.
Method
  1. Cleaned and classified facilities. Only 28 are free public facilities; most are private, fee-paying clinics.
  2. Measured every population hexagon and site against its nearest public facility in UTM 36S, counting facilities up to 15 km over the district line.
  3. Flagged drone-priority sites: no regular health service and no vehicle access.
  4. Modelled a 20 km drone network from Queen Elizabeth Central Hospital, then tested every public hospital and health centre as a second hub.
QGIS / GDALPythonShapelyLeafletNetwork design
What it shows

On a map most sites look fine, with a median of just 2.8 km to a public facility. The survey shows otherwise: 45 sites had no regular health service, and 68 could not be reached by vehicle. Of the 26 sites with both problems, 16 fall within 20 km of QECH. A second hub at Chitera Health Centre, just east of the district, covers the other 10.

Recommendation

Launch drone deliveries of malaria tests and treatment, ORS and lab-sample returns from QECH to the 16 priority sites first. Then assess Chitera Health Centre's cold-chain and crew capacity to open a second hub.

Limits
  • Distances are straight-line; travel time comes from a modelled raster.
  • The registry's "functional" status field is out of date, and 44 Blantyre facilities have no coordinates.
  • The displacement data is a single survey round.
